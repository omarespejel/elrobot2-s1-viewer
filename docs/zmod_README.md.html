Commissioning ramp (T8x4, 4 mm lead, brake motor on 24 V)

  1. Electrical bring-up first (zmod_electrical.md section 6): 24.0 V supply, motor current 0.5 A, brake test, E-stop test. Check limit-switch polarity (normally closed) and the lower/upper stops by hand.
  2. First run 15 mm/s (225 rpm, 12 000 1/16-microsteps/s), no payload, full stroke twice.
  3. Ramp 15 -> 25 -> 40 mm/s (375 / 600 rpm; 20 000 / 32 000 microsteps/s). At each step run 5 full strokes with the 2.13 kg payload, then empty. Accept the speed if the home switch repeats within 0.1 mm over the 5 cycles, there is no stall or audible resonance band and the motor case stays below 70 C. 24 V is required above ~28 mm/s (12 V gives no torque at 40 mm/s). Do NOT go to 56 or 60 mm/s.
  4. Stop at the first failing speed and set the cruise speed to 0.75 x the last good one. Acceleration 150 mm/s^2. Sweep 100-400 rpm slowly once to find stepper or screw resonance bands.
  5. Always stop with a speed ramp BEFORE the brake sets; never run unattended before the brake and detent tests of section 6 of the electrical page have passed.

Open issues and items to verify on hardware

  • Driver current 1.4 A rms ASSUMED: TMC2209 modules are only rated about 1.2 A rms without heatsink/airflow (margin 2.8 instead of 3.2).
  • 4040 profile: two-slots-per-face V-slot type required; second moment 7.5-9.9 cm4 by maker (forward lean alone 2.0-2.7 mm); rod joint stiffness (0.03-0.1 N/um) and foot compliance not measured; side rod works for +x lean only; rod ringing 4-9 Hz at 20-100 N pretension not analysed.
  • Printed clamps and anchors are hand-checked only; print one of each and load the rods to ~260 N before trusting them. M5 bolts through 18 mm plywood (anchors, feet) are UNVERIFIED hardware.
  • Plywood density (650 kg/m3) and ballast placement are estimates; the dynamic case governs the ballast (6.6 kg rear half / 10.8 kg centre).
  • VERIFY: T8x4 nut flange PCD and hole phase, KW12-3 hole pitch and lever travel (>= 3 mm overtravel), 608 seat fit, coupler bore depth (>= 12 mm each side), Gemini 2 tripod hole offset and FOV, stepper torque curve at 24 V, screw root diameter (5.5-6.2 mm), turret mass (estimated 0.555 kg), motor STEP from OMC (CAD uses a 42.3 x 42.3 x 79 mm envelope).
